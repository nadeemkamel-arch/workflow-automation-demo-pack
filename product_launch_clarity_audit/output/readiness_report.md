# Product Launch Clarity Audit: TripNest Lite

Category: travel planning web app
Stage: private beta landing page plus clickable prototype
Audience: busy solo travelers planning two-to-five day city trips
Business goal: collect ten qualified beta users without confusing first-time visitors

Readiness score: 47/100
Decision: not ready for broad launch; ready for a small private test after quick wins.
