Before You Apply

1. Know Your Constraints

  • Location radius and transportation.
  • Days and hours available.
  • Earliest start date and last summer availability date.
  • Limits around lifting, standing, outdoor work, weekends, or commute.

2. Pick Role Categories

  • Camp, recreation, youth sports, or activity assistant.
  • Retail, food service, cafe, host, cashier, or server.
  • Front desk, office assistant, tutoring, event staff, or customer service.

3. Build A Simple Resume

  • Use school, jobs, volunteering, clubs, sports, projects, or family responsibilities.
  • Write bullets around responsibility, reliability, service, teamwork, and follow-through.
  • Avoid fake experience, inflated titles, or skills you cannot discuss.

4. Apply In Batches

  • Start with 8-12 applications.
  • Mix quick applications with better-fit roles.
  • Track date applied, company, role, and follow-up date.

Interview Prep

  • Prepare a short answer for: "Tell me about yourself."
  • Know your real availability before the interview.
  • Have one story about responsibility, conflict, teamwork, or a busy situation.
  • Ask about pay, training, schedule, supervision, and start date before accepting.

Safety And Privacy

Do not send strangers Social Security numbers, bank details, ID photos, payroll forms, or passwords. For minors, involve a parent or guardian.
