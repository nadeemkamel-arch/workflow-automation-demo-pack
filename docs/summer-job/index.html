The checklist

Four steps before you apply

1

Set your search

  • Write down your work radius, transportation, start date, and weekly availability.
  • Pick two or three role categories.
  • Track the company, role, date applied, and follow-up date.
2

Build your resume

  • Use school, volunteering, sports, clubs, projects, caregiving, or paid work.
  • Show responsibility, service, teamwork, and reliability.
  • Keep it to one clear page.
3

Apply in batches

  • Start with 8-12 roles, mixing quick applications with better-fit opportunities.
  • Adjust your top bullets and skills to each role.
  • Follow up after 5-7 business days when contact information is available.
4

Prepare to talk

  • Practice a short answer to: Tell me about yourself.
  • Prepare one story about responsibility, teamwork, or a busy situation.
  • Know your availability and ask about pay, training, schedule, and start date.

Privacy check

Do not send strangers your Social Security number, bank details, ID photos, payroll forms, or passwords. Confirm the employer and website before sharing personal information. For minors, involve a parent or guardian.
